## Dependency coverage

The nontrivial short-time gradient bound is verified in Sturm's theorem and independently located as (GE) in CFM's identical geometric setup. The other ingredients are the Neumann heat kernel's symmetry, conservation, smoothing and strong continuity, operator-domain commutation, Sobolev locality and smooth approximation, and Hilbert-space weak compactness/lower semicontinuity. They are stated explicitly in the manuscript; the detailed pre-submission argument check is in [RESOLUTION_REPORT.md](RESOLUTION_REPORT.md).

The current catalogue also cites Cancès-Monsaingeon-Natale for broader chain-rule motivation. This additional proof does not use that paper or make a claim about its separate rough-domain question, so its bibliography is not copied into the manuscript as an unused dependency.
